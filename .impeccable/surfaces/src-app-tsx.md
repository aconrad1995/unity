# Marketplace (shop, listing, sell, saved)

Scope: the resale marketplace screens of Unity. Mode: Operate. Mobile-first.
Audience and job: a buyer with an event coming up, hunting a specific brand, size or style on their phone. Second: a seller listing a lightly worn outfit in under a minute.
Constraints: sample listings only and labelled as sample; no real checkout, accounts or chat yet; never generic-thrift, corporate, sketchy, or hard to search.
Unresolved: real photos, payments, accounts, final name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A shop that feels like getting ready with friends: a soft sky you browse in, where every outfit states brand, size, condition and price up front. Refuses the dark-with-neon rave default and the plain white thrift grid.

OWN-WORLD: Sky-blue ground, bubblegum-pink header with a scalloped cloud edge, white cloud cards, plum ink text, hot-pink actions, gold four-point sparkles. Bubble-shaped filter chips. Rounded bubbly display type (Fredoka) over a rounded text face (Nunito). Authored line icons, no emoji.

STORY: The buyer sees outfits at once, taps a brand bubble or types a search, trusts the condition badge, opens a listing, saves or buys. Selling is one short form with big obvious choices.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pink header with the Unity wordmark and a full-width search field; cloud edge below; a scrolling row of brand bubbles; category chips and size/sort selects; a two-column grid of listings with photo, price bubble, brand, size and condition badge; a three-item bottom bar (Shop, Sell, Saved) with Sell as the raised pink button.

SIGNATURE: saving an outfit bursts a small ring of sparkles from the heart; the cloud edge and bubbles carry the world everywhere else.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). "Pinks, blues, bubbles or clouds, sparkles, glitter, cutesy design with dummy proof functionality."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
