# Real-phone check (about 10 minutes)

Do this on an iPhone, and on an Android phone if one is handy. You need two devices for step 4,
for example your phone and Mary's, or your phone and your laptop. Write down anything that looks off
and send me a screenshot.

## 1. Sign in (Safari, not the home screen app yet)

- [ ] Open unityrave.com. The shop loads with no blank screen.
- [ ] You → Account → sign in with your email and password. Your name and listings appear.
- [ ] Close Safari fully, reopen unityrave.com. You're still signed in.

## 2. Add to home screen

- [ ] In Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. The icon looks right and the name says Unity.
- [ ] Open Unity from the home screen. It opens full screen, with no Safari address bar.
- [ ] The home screen app keeps its own sign-in, so sign in again here. Your listings appear.
- [ ] Rotate the phone, then scroll the shop. Nothing gets cut off at the notch or the home bar.

## 3. Turn notifications on (from the home screen app)

- [ ] You → Phone notifications card → **Turn notifications on**. iPhone asks to allow notifications. Tap Allow.
- [ ] The card now says **Turn notifications off**.

## 4. Get a notification

- [ ] Lock the phone that turned notifications on.
- [ ] From the other device, signed in as someone else, message that person about an outfit.
- [ ] Within a minute, the locked phone shows "*Their name*: About *outfit*…".
- [ ] Tap the notification. Unity opens straight into that chat.

## 5. Sell something (Mary's phone is best)

- [ ] Sell → Tap to add photos → pick 3 from the camera roll. They show in order, the first marked as cover.
- [ ] Fill it in and tap **List my outfit**. The outfit page opens with "Your outfit is listed".
- [ ] **List another outfit**. The shipping speed is already picked.
- [ ] On the outfit page: Edit photos or details → move a photo to cover → Save. The new cover shows at once, in the shop too.
- [ ] When done, tap **Remove my listing** on the test outfit.

## 6. Forgot password

- [ ] Sign out. Tap **Forgot your password?** and enter your email. The email arrives within a minute and isn't in spam.
- [ ] Tap the link in the email. It opens Unity and lets you set a new password, then you're signed in.

Anything that fails: note the step number, the phone and iOS/Android version, and what you saw.
